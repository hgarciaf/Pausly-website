Privacy Policy for Pausly

Last updated: April 26, 2026

Overview

Pausly is a focus timer app for macOS. Your privacy is important to us.

Data Collection

Pausly does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All your data stays locally on your Mac.

What Pausly Stores Locally

  • Timer session history
  • Focus statistics and streaks
  • Achievement progress
  • App usage data (when tracking is enabled)
  • Your preferences and settings

All of this data is stored in your Mac's Application Support folder and never leaves your device.

Accessibility Permission

Pausly requests Accessibility permission to track which apps you use during focus sessions. This data is processed locally and is never sent anywhere.

Third-Party Services

Pausly does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact: support@pausly.xyz
